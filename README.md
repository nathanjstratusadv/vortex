# Vortex DB

The string in and out database you never needed!

## Why?

Working with information, maintaining data structure and relations is too much overhead. 
You should be able to word vomit in as you please and request that word vomit back as more of a word salad.

## Seriously Why?

I have always wanted to make a database and I wanted to do something different while learning a new programming 
language!

## What or Who is this for?

It's for people who do not want to deal with setting up complex structure for their projects and just want to get to 
work.
This database is also designed to be super easy for agentic artificial intelligence to use.

## Getting Started

Download the executable for your operating system and run it, it will expose a port and you can send all your words 
there and read the responses as you want.
